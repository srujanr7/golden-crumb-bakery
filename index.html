Our story

A small oven, a big neighbourhood.

Golden Crumb started with one wood-fired oven and a sourdough starter we still feed every day. Ten years on, we're still a family bakery that believes good bread takes time.

Every loaf is shaped by hand, and anything left at closing goes to the community food bank.
